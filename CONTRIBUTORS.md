## How recognition works

- **Every contribution counts** — code, docs, tests, triage, reviews, design,
  and community support. The
  [all-contributors](https://allcontributors.org/) spirit applies even though
  we track recognition manually here.
- **When your PR merges**, a maintainer adds you to the Contributors list
  above (or add yourself in the same PR). New contributors are also called out
  in the release notes for the release that includes their work.
- **This file is reviewed every release** so no contribution goes
  unrecognized. If you contributed and aren't listed, open a PR or a short
  issue and we'll fix it.
